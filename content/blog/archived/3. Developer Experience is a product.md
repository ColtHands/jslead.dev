Developer experience is a product.

It exhibits everything a product can be - users, stakeholders, features, processes, satisfaction gap, metrics, roadmaps, a vision.

[A product is a vehicle to deliver value. A product could be a service, a physical product or something more abstract.](https://www.scrum.org/resources/what-product-1)

Developer Experience or Engineering Productivity does not enforce rules, it is not a mechanism of control.
A developer that is forced to be productive by working faster, using a specific tool, or following a specific process will not produce more value to their users.

A developer that is enabled to do their best job will almost always produce more value to their users.
